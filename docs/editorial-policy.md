# Editorial decision policy

No candidate is automatically approved. Approval requires a different photo and
appearance, compatible chronology, visual identity of required garment details,
Grade A/B source evidence, no contradictions, usable rights status, and a written
editor rationale. Similar colour, designer, silhouette, or accessories are not
proof of the same garment.

The editorial unit is one outfit, not one Reddit post or one celebrity. Panel
order is: today's appearance, an earlier appearance by the same person, earlier
appearances by other people, then an official designer-model reference. Current
post angles replace historical panels when no historical match is defensible.
Official designer imagery supports garment identity but does not independently
prove that a celebrity wore that exact physical item.

Allowed final labels are `same_item_same_celebrity`,
`same_outfit_same_celebrity`, `same_item_other_celebrity`, and
`same_outfit_other_celebrity`. Duplicate photos/events, `similar_not_same`, and
`insufficient_evidence` cannot produce a final collage.
