# Source and rights policy

- Manual Reddit URL intake is enabled; scheduled access remains disabled until
  approved API access passes a seven-day proof period.
- Reddit and repost accounts are discovery sources, never identity proof.
- Grade A/B identity evidence is mandatory for approval.
- Every displayed asset retains its canonical source and rights status.
- Designer-model imagery must come from the designer's official campaign,
  lookbook, collection, or product page and must match the exact garment or
  outfit. Similar designs are not eligible collage panels.
- Store the official URL, publisher/designer, visible credit, retrieval date,
  source grade, and rights status for every designer reference.
- `do_not_use` assets are blocked from collage rendering.
- A source URL is not a publishing licence; the editorial owner must confirm
  image-use and credit policy before launch.
